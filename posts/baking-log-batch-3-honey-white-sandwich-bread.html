Baking Log: Batch #3 (Honey White Sandwich Bread)

tags:#blog #bread-log #diy #honey

01. introduction

It's been two weeks, time for another bread session!

I was out of King Arthur flour this time, so I opted for 100% Hudson Cream—no mix like last time.

[fig: Hudson ~~cat~~ Cream Flour]

I've been wanting to experiment with the difference sugar vs honey makes in the mix, but using a different flour this time meant I didn't want to add more variables. It's kind of a science. So I used honey.

The Specs

  • Recipe: Homemade Bread Recipe - Tastes Better from Scratch
  • Flour: 100% Hudson Cream Flour (6 cups total)
  • Sweetener: Honey
  • Salt: 365 Coarse Sea Salt

The Bake & Result: 9/10

An inadvertent variable this time was the second rise. I was unable to use the oven bread proofer (it was in-use for another project), so I just relied on room temperature, which is pretty inconsistent. Despite the variable, the rise came out well.

[fig: Second rise]

30 minutes in the oven at 350 degrees and voilà.

[fig: Out of the oven and buttered]

Crumb shot on the board:

[fig: Near perfection slices]

The verdict: 9/10. This batch turned out nicely! The crumb is perfect—just the right texture, size, and density.
