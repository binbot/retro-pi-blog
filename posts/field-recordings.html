field recording in the quiet woods

tags:#audio #nature #field-recordings

01. capturing quietness

there is something magical about walking deep into the woods at 5:00 AM, setting up a couple of sensitive microphones, and just listening.

in this session, my goal was to record the transition from night-time silence to the morning bird chorus. the woods behind my home are perfect for this, as they are relatively isolated from highway noise.

02. the gear

you don't need multi-thousand dollar equipment to start, but low self-noise preamps are essential for quiet environments:

  • recorder: Zoom H5 (using the onboard X/Y capsules).
  • mics: Matched pair of LOM Usi Pro omnidirectional microphones.
  • wind protection: Rycote baby ball gags (critical for even the slightest breeze).

i recorded at 24-bit / 96kHz to allow for high dynamic range and potential pitch manipulation in post-production.

03. the results

you can listen to a short 2-minute excerpt of the forest rain recording in the audio archives section. notice the gentle patter on the pine needles and the distant owl calls near the beginning.
