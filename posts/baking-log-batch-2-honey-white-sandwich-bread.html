Baking Log: Batch #2 (Honey White Sandwich Bread)

tags:#blog #bread-log #diy #honey

01. introduction

It’s been two weeks, time for another bread session!

In the last post, I mentioned experimenting with sugar versus honey next time around. But with the mixed flour already introducing a new variable, I stuck with honey again.

[fig: Measured cup of honey]

The Specs

  • Recipe: Homemade Bread Recipe - Tastes Better from Scratch
  • Flour: 50% King Arthur Unbleached Flour / 50% Hudson Cream Flour (6 cups total)
  • Sweetener: Honey
  • Salt: 365 Coarse Sea Salt

The Bake & Result: 8/10

The rise went well this time around, though the tops flattened out just a bit while waiting on the oven to finish preheating.

[fig: Second rise in pans]

Despite that, the bake turned out nicely! Brushed both crusts with butter right after pulling them.

[fig: Aerial view of both buttered loaves]

[fig: Front view of loaf]

Crumb shot once cut:

[fig: Sliced bread on cutting board]

Taste is great, though no change in rating from last time. A solid bake overall—8/10.
