## 01. introduction

Time for another batch. An excellent morning for baking with sunny skies and mild temps.

Same ingredients as last time—100% Hudson Cream flour—but this time I finally swapped in sugar instead of honey, as noted in [previous](https://binbot.dev/posts/baking-log-batch-2-honey-white-sandwich-bread.html) [posts](https://binbot.dev/posts/baking-log-batch-3-honey-white-sandwich-bread.html).

The isolated variable this round was sugar instead of honey. While it didn't seem to have much effect on the final product, I did notice both the first and second rise took a little longer than usual. No worries, though—the second rise ended up looking promising:

![Second rise](/media/second_rise3.jpg)

### The Specs
- **Recipe:** [Homemade Bread Recipe - Tastes Better from Scratch](https://tastesbetterfromscratch.com/bread-recipe/)
- **Flour:** 100% [Hudson Cream Flour](https://www.hudsoncream.com/) (6 cups total)
- **Sweetener:** Granulated sugar
- **Salt:** 365 Coarse Sea Salt

### The Bake & Result

And as always, a sweet sigh of relief when they turn out well.
