## 01. introduction

It’s been two weeks, time for another bread session!

In the [last post](https://binbot.dev/blog/baking-log-batch-1/), I mentioned experimenting with sugar versus honey next time around. But with the mixed flour already introducing a new variable, I stuck with honey again.

![Measured cup of honey](./media/quarter_cup_honey.jpg)
### The Specs
- **Recipe:** [Homemade Bread Recipe - Tastes Better from Scratch](https://tastesbetterfromscratch.com/bread-recipe/)
- **Flour:** 50% [King Arthur Unbleached Flour](https://shop.kingarthurbaking.com/) / 50% [Hudson Cream Flour](https://www.hudsoncream.com/) (6 cups total)
- **Sweetener:** Honey
- **Salt:** 365 Coarse Sea Salt

### The Bake & Result: 8/10

The rise went well this time around, though the tops flattened out just a bit while waiting on the oven to finish preheating.

![Second rise in pans](/media/second_in_pans.jpg)

Despite that, the bake turned out nicely! Brushed both crusts with butter right after pulling them.

![Aerial view of both buttered loaves](/media/aerial.jpg)
![Front view of loaf](/media/side_view.jpg)

Crumb shot once cut:

![Sliced bread on cutting board](/media/slices.jpg)

Taste is great, though no change in rating from last time. A solid bake overall—8/10.
