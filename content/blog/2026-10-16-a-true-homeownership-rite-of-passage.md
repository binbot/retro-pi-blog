## 01. Introduction

It had been really stinking in the house for about three days. 

At first, it started like that kind of smell you get in the kitchen, but we couldn't find anything. I took out the trash. We looked all around and made sure there wasn't any spoiled food in the fridge or in the cabinets or anything like that. 

It just kind of kept smelling. Then the smell started moving into the middle of the house, like around the dining room, and we were left wondering what on earth it could be.

As the days went on, the smell got worse. I opened some windows. You wouldn't smell it so much upstairs, maybe just an occasional whiff, but downstairs was like: whoa. You could really smell it. I left the back door open to air things out, and finally April and I were talking about it. We realized: you know what? Either we've got some sort of dead animal underneath the house in the crawl space, or something is seriously wrong.

## 02. Gearing Up for the Depths

Then today came. We woke up, and as soon as we got out of bed, we could smell it immediately. It hit us right away: *Oh my god, we've got to do something about this.*

So I suited up:

* Long sleeves and heavy pants
* Sturdy boot-type shoes
* A hat
* Headlamp strapped on
* Phone flashlight ready for backup
* A good old-fashioned shovel

I got down on my hands and knees, then lay flat on my stomach, and crawled into the crawl space.

## 03. Into the Dark

That crawl space is dark. There are no lights down there at all. It's just a dirt floor covered in plastic tarps, very dirty, with old insulation hanging down everywhere. It is seriously hard to see anything. In only a few spots can you even manage to stay on your hands and knees. In other spots, you have to be completely flat on your stomach. 

The underside of the house is essentially divided into two sections:

1. **The First Chamber:** From the crawl space access door, you get just a tiny sliver of daylight, but still plenty of hanging insulation and dirt to navigate.
2. **The Deep Void:** Once you squeeze through the partition into that second section, it is pitch black. 

And of course, that second area is right where the critter decided to go.

I spent time searching around the first area and couldn't find a thing. I was starting to get worried that I wouldn't be able to track it down at all. But then I pushed into the second part of the house, right into one of the absolute hardest spots to reach. The ceiling is so low that I could only get to it on my stomach. At best, I could prop myself up slightly on my elbows, but mostly I was just dragging myself along flat.

## 04. Face-to-Face with the Beast

And there he was. A big old possum. Dead as a doornail, and really, really stinking. 

Who knows what happened to the poor fellow, but man, it was gross. Working in that cramped pocket, I somehow managed to use the shovel to lift and nudge the poor guy onto a big plastic heavy-duty garbage bag. 

Then came the hard part: laying flat on my stomach, scootching backward along the plastic ground, dragging the dead carcass of this stinking beast behind me. The guy weighed at least five or six pounds. He was pretty big, at least the size of a hefty cat or a small dog. Amazingly, he mostly stayed balanced on that plastic bag while I dragged him out. I still can't believe he didn't slide off constantly, though I did have to pause and use the shovel a couple of times to push him back onto the plastic.

Finally, I made it to the light, hauled everything out, scooped him securely into the bag with the shovel, tied it tight, and tossed it into the trash can outside.

## 05. Mission Accomplished

I have never done anything like this in my entire life. Absolutely crazy. 

Let me tell you, that crawl space is definitely on the scary side. Being somewhere with zero freedom of movement, stuck in the pitch dark, knowing full well something dead (and maybe something alive) is in there with you... it is not a great feeling.

Looking back on it now, I'm proud of myself for actually going down there and getting it done:

* The house already smells a whole lot better
* No more mystery odors lingering in the hallway
* I now know the exact layout of the crawl space if I ever have to venture back in

Job well done. We get to enjoy a fresh, clean-smelling house for the weekend.
